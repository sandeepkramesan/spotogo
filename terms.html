1. What SpotoGo does

SpotoGo reads the public caption or description of reels you share with it, suggests the restaurant and its location, and saves spots on your phone. Suggestions come from public text and open map data, so they can be wrong: check a place before you travel there.

2. Your use

  • Use SpotoGo for personal, lawful purposes.
  • Respect the creators whose reels you share. SpotoGo credits them, plays their reels through Instagram’s and YouTube’s own players, and doesn’t copy their videos.
  • Don’t misuse the app or its services, for example by trying to claim a restaurant that isn’t yours.

SpotoGo’s app, servers, website, place data and the way SpotoGo finds places are ours (ftwco.’s). You may use them only through the SpotoGo app and website, as intended. You must not, and must not help anyone else to:

3. Your account

You need an account to use SpotoGo. Log in with an email address you control, or with Google. Keep access to that email safe: anyone who can read it can log in to your account. You can log out of all phones or delete your account at any time in the app. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service.

4. Your profile and public spots

Your username, name and public spots can be seen by anyone on SpotoGo. Don’t pick a username that impersonates someone else, and don’t post public spots, names or notes that are unlawful, hateful or misleading. Ratings are personal opinions. We may change a username or remove public content that breaks these rules, and verified badges are given only by SpotoGo.

4a. Comments

You can comment on public spots. Comments are text only: no links or images. Don’t post anything that belongs to someone else, is obscene, pornographic or harmful to children, invades someone’s privacy, harasses, defames or threatens anyone, promotes hatred or violence, impersonates someone, deceives or misleads (including fake or paid reviews), infringes a copyright or trademark, contains software viruses, or breaks any law in force in India. A word filter and a limit on how many comments you can post apply. The owner of a spot, and a restaurant on its own place, can hide comments there; anyone can report one. We remove comments that break these rules, may suspend accounts that keep breaking them, and keep removed comments for 180 days as the law requires.

5. Restaurant profiles

Only a restaurant’s owner, manager or staff acting for it may claim its official page, using the restaurant’s own GST number. We check each claim by hand and may refuse it or remove a page that was claimed improperly. Restaurants are responsible for the details they publish.
