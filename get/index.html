Install in four steps

  1. Tap Download for AndroidYour phone saves the file Spotogo.apk.
  2. Open the fileFrom the download notification or your Downloads. If Android asks, let your browser install apps (Install unknown apps → Allow), then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns youSpotoGo isn’t on the Play Store yet, so Google doesn’t know it. Tap More details → Install anyway.
  4. Open SpotoGo and sign inTap Continue with Google, choose your name, then enter your invite code.

Good to know

  • Android only for now. An iPhone version comes later.
  • Your spots are saved to your account, so they’re safe if you change phones.
  • New version? Download it from this page again and install it over the old one. Nothing is lost.
  • Something not working, or an idea? Write to support@spotogo.in.
