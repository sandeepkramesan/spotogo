Your SpotoGo account and spots

Your account holds your email address (and Google ID and name if you log in with Google), your username, your saved spots and notes (public and private), who you follow, and alert settings.

  • In the app: Profile → your initial (top right) → Delete account. You’re logged out on every phone at once, and 30 days later we permanently delete your account, username, every saved spot, your lists, your follows and followers, and, for restaurant accounts, your claims and official pages. Changed your mind? Log in again within those 30 days and nothing is deleted.
  • Just some spots: delete them in the app (long-press to select several). The deletion syncs to your account and your other phones.
  • By email: write to privacy@spotogo.in from the address you log in with. We delete the account within 30 days.

Logging out or uninstalling SpotoGo only removes the copy on that phone; your account stays until you delete it.

Instagram and YouTube

SpotoGo doesn’t connect to Instagram or YouTube accounts and keeps no data from them. Connections made while that was being tested were deleted on 28 October 2026. If you still see SpotoGo under Instagram’s “Apps and websites” or in your Google Account permissions, you can remove it there.
